## 1. Example data

Published at `https://ksatohds.github.io/webR/CART/data/` (source: `site/CART/data/`).

| File | Data | R package | Package licence | Primary source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `titanic.csv` | `Titanic` | datasets | Part of R (GPL-2 \| GPL-3) | Dawson (1995), *Journal of Statistics Education* 3(3), doi:10.1080/10691898.1995.11910499; data originally collected by the British Board of Trade inquiry into the sinking (1912) |
| `iris.csv` | `iris` | datasets | Part of R (GPL-2 \| GPL-3) | Fisher (1936), *Annals of Eugenics* 7(2), 179–188, doi:10.1111/j.1469-1809.1936.tb02137.x; data collected by Anderson (1935), *Bulletin of the American Iris Society* 59, 2–5 |

Changes:

- `iris` — none.
- `Titanic` — the 4-way frequency table (2,201 people) is expanded to one row per person,
  and `Survived` is replaced by `Died` (1 = did not survive). No information is added or
  removed: cross-tabulating the CSV reproduces `datasets::Titanic` exactly. Rows carry no
  identifiers, and their order (grouped by category) has no meaning. Dawson (1995), the source
  cited in R's `?Titanic`, likewise provides one record per person on board.

Both files are generated by `data-raw/CART_example_data.R` (R 4.4.1). Bibliographic records
of the sources are in `data-raw/references/`.
