Abstract:
  None (the 1936 paper has no abstract). The abstract field of the Crossref record holds
  Wiley's note on making the Annals of Eugenics (1925-1954) available as a historical archive.
  Summary: using the length and width of the sepals and petals of 50 flowers from each of three
  iris species (Iris setosa, versicolor, virginica), the paper discusses discriminating the
  species with a linear discriminant function.

Use in this repository:
  Source of site/CART/data/iris.csv (R's datasets::iris written to CSV unchanged).
  The data were collected by Anderson (1935); see Anderson1935.txt.

Related:

  as the correct ones (checked 2026-10-04).
